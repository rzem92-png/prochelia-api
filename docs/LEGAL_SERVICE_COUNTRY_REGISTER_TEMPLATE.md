## Decision rules

1. BLOCKED if legal classification is unknown, required permit is absent, insurance does not expressly cover the activity/territory, provider eligibility is unverified, or the contracts do not match actual operations.
2. CONDITIONAL only for a documented, legally permissible limited pilot with controls, capped exposure, named owner and expiry date.
3. APPROVED only after all applicable evidence is present, current and independently reviewed.
4. A new task, beneficiary, territory, vehicle use, provider status, payment flow or AI/algorithmic feature creates a new review requirement.
5. No AI system may convert BLOCKED to APPROVED or waive a missing permit, qualification, insurance or human sign-off.
